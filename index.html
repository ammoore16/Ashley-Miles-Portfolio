My approach

Good care begins with understanding.

Nutrition is deeply personal. The best care recognizes the whole person—their health, their circumstances, their culture, their experiences, and what matters to them.

In December 2026, I expect to graduate from the University of New England with a Master of Science in Applied Nutrition, Dietetics Focus Area. Upon successful completion of the program, I will have completed more than 1,000 hours of supervised experiential learning and will be eligible to take the registration examination for dietitians.

UNE’s dietetics pathway integrates graduate coursework with supervised experience in clinical nutrition, community nutrition, counseling, and food systems.

One of the most meaningful parts of my education has been completing supervised experiential learning in both Colorado and Mississippi. Training in two states introduced me to different communities, health care systems, and approaches to nutrition practice.

My experiences have included acute and critical care, eating disorder treatment, neurorehabilitation, WIC and public health programming, school nutrition, food service management, and lifestyle medicine.

Supervised experiential learning

Learning through service, practice, and collaboration.

My training has taken place across clinical, community, public health, and food systems environments in Colorado and Mississippi.

Jan. 2026 — Present
University of Mississippi Medical Center
Jackson, Mississippi

Rotated through surgical, medical, cardiac, neurological, and burn intensive care settings while building confidence in complex, high-acuity nutrition care.

Conducted nutrition-focused physical exams, developed individualized care plans and PES statements, interpreted biochemical and anthropometric data, and calculated enteral and parenteral nutrition support.

May — Sept. 2026
Rankin County School District
Office of Child Nutrition

Worked across child nutrition operations, procurement, annual budgeting, staffing, federal and state funding requirements, menu standards, vendor relationships, and board proposals.

Developed a year-to-date expenditure tracking model and introduced a second breakfast pilot program for Richland High School.

Jan. 2024 — Apr. 2025
Adams County Health Department
Colorado

Conducted a weight inclusivity environmental scan and supported WIC initiatives, breastfeeding resources, grant planning, diabetes education, community health programming, farmers markets, and food access efforts.

Presented research on national WIC online ordering pilots and participant-centered implementation strategies.

July 2024
Craig Hospital
Englewood, Colorado

Provided nutrition care in an inpatient neurorehabilitation environment, including patients with traumatic brain injuries and spinal cord injuries.

Conducted assessments and nutrition-focused physical exams, calculated enteral nutrition requirements, and developed individualized interventions addressing hypermetabolism, neurogenic bowel concerns, weight loss, ostomies, hydration, and increased protein needs.

Jan. — June 2024
UCHealth Parkview Medical Center
Pueblo, Colorado

Applied the Nutrition Care Process across hospital units including intensive care, neuro, oncology, and rehabilitation.

Used Epic to evaluate clinical data, developed PES statements and interventions, educated patients and families, and gained experience with tube feeding, TPN, medication interactions, and disease pathophysiology.

Jan. — June 2024
Colorado Charter School Institute
Denver, Colorado

Developed food safety and allergen management training, specialized menus for diverse dietary needs, food code education, Civil Rights training, and resources covering food allergies, religious dietary practices, and the USDA Food Buying Guide.

July 2023 — Sept. 2024
ACUTE Center for Eating Disorders
Denver, Colorado

Supported nutrition rehabilitation in a specialized high-acuity inpatient setting serving individuals with severe eating disorders and complications of malnutrition.

Collaborated with registered dietitians, nursing, mental-health professionals, and the broader treatment team while supporting structured meal service, food substitutions, allergy management, therapeutic consistency, and safe nutrition restoration.
